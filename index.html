Welcome

Experience that brings perspective. Care that stays personal.

Stephanie Santora is a Licensed Independent Clinical Social Worker with over 25 years of experience providing psychotherapy to adults through individual and couples counseling.

Her earlier work in community mental health and hospital settings shaped a broad clinical foundation. Today, she offers a thoughtful and collaborative space for people navigating anxiety, depression, self-esteem concerns, relationship challenges, grief, and other significant seasons of life.

The approach

A balance of reflection and practical movement.

Therapy with Stephanie is grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and mindfulness-based work. Together, you can look closely at patterns, build useful tools, and strengthen the self-awareness that supports lasting change.

Emotionally focused and strength-based perspectives help keep the work responsive—to your relationships, your existing capacities, and the goals that matter to you.
